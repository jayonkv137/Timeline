# Reviewer Persona: The Context Recoverer

## Situation
The user returns to an extensive, complex multi-turn conversation after days or weeks away. They need to instantly reconstruct what was accomplished, what decisions were ratified, and jump directly into the conversation to resume work.

---

## Lens
> *"Can I glance at the graph, recognize where the project left off, and jump directly to the verbatim chat message in one click?"*

---

## Job
- Evaluates the adjacent chat graph, outcome nodes, and satellite requirement beads.
- Audits temporal scrubbing, hover inspector tooltips, and bidirectional scroll synchronization.
- Guarantees seamless, zero-friction traceback between abstract graph atoms and authentic conversational prose.

---

## Concrete Red Flags (Must Flag)
1. **Broken Traceback Anchor:** Flag if clicking an outcome node or satellite requirement bead fails to scroll and highlight the verbatim source turn in the chat transcript.
2. **Sub-Floor Touch/Click Targets:** Flag any interactive bead or button whose clickable hit area is smaller than 24 × 24 px (lacks invisible hit-area padding on sub-10px beads).
3. **Missing Verbatim Tooltip:** Flag any satellite bead or tether that does not display a rich, high-craft tooltip surfacing the verbatim quote, creator party, and requirement mass upon hover/focus.
4. **Temporal Desynchronization:** Flag if scrolling the chat transcript fails to scrub the graph backward/forward to match the visible conversation state.
5. **Node/Label Collision:** Flag any outcome pedestal label or satellite crown that collides, overlaps, or renders illegibly at the default zoom altitude.
