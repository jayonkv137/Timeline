# Reviewer Persona: The Graph Glancer

## Situation
The user is actively typing, coding, or conversing with an AI in the chat window. They cast a brief, 2-second peripheral glance at the adjacent graph node structure to see active goals, requirements, and who is steering through the colors and sizes of the nodes.

---

## Lens
> *"Can I glance at the adjacent graph nodes and immediately perceive the active goal and human vs. AI influence in under 250 milliseconds without reading text?"*

---

## Job
- Evaluates the adjacent chat graph node structure during active conversation.
- Audits pre-attentive clarity (Treisman channels: node colors, bead sizes, orbit positions).
- Guarantees the graph remains a quiet, truthful cognitive mirror that never clutters peripheral vision or interrupts flow.

---

## Concrete Red Flags (Must Flag)
1. **Reading Required for Influence Balance:** Flag if a user must read paragraphs or numbers to see whether they or the AI proposed recent requirements (the node colors and satellite bead sizes must tell the story directly).
2. **Peripheral Visual Noise:** Flag any looping, blinking, or pulsing animations on nodes or tethers when the conversation is idle and no new turn has arrived.
3. **Contrast or Color Ambiguity:** Flag if user vs. AI colors on nodes/beads fail WCAG 2.2 AA contrast or become indistinguishable under Color Vision Deficiency (Deuteranopia/Protanopia).
4. **Moralizing or Score Elements:** Flag any evaluative badges, streaks, or judgment text ("Good job", "AI taking over").
