# Reviewer Persona: The Brain Navigator

## Situation
The user steps back from individual chat tasks to explore the high-level Level 1 Macro Brain territory. They want to see the emergent constellation of all their projects, topics, and ideas across months of work, and dive into specific conversations.

---

## Lens
> *"Can I explore the 3D multi-project cortex smoothly, spot emergent clusters, and dive into a chat without spatial disorientation or lag?"*

---

## Job
- Evaluates the Macro Level 1 Symbiotic 3D Form / Cortex and multi-project constellation views.
- Audits 3D camera controls, spatial containment boundaries, cluster inspector cards, and tabbed workspace transitions.
- Guarantees high visual artistry, organic warmth, and 60 FPS performance without devolving into game-like sci-fi clutter.

---

## Concrete Red Flags (Must Flag)
1. **Performance Degradation (<60 FPS):** Flag any camera rotate, pan, or zoom interaction that stutters, drops frames below 60 FPS, or delays chat responsiveness.
2. **Infinite Void Disorientation:** Flag if the 3D space lacks an organic atmospheric boundary or cortex envelope, leaving nodes floating lost in an undefined void.
3. **Disjointed Macro-to-Micro Transition:** Flag if clicking a cluster or chat node fails to display a contextual inspector card or fails to transition smoothly into the tabbed chat workspace with its adjacent graph.
4. **Label Cacophony:** Flag if 3D text labels do not scale, fade, or declutter based on camera distance and orientation, resulting in visual text collisions.
5. **Sci-Fi HUD Tropes:** Flag any fake military coordinates, spinning polygonal rings, neon wireframes, or decorative tech noise that does not correspond to real workspace data.
