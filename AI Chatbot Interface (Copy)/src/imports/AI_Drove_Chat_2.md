# CHAT 2 — "AI DROVE"  (offloaded / do-it-all)

SCENARIO: someone offloads a whole task — a cover letter — to the AI. Vague input,
the AI produces everything, the user only makes small nudges ("longer", "catchier"),
brings no material of their own, takes the output as-is, and even waves off the one
suggestion to improve it. The first AI turn is the big moment — it invents the whole
artifact.

EXPECTED WAVEFORM SILHOUETTE: top-heavy and ORANGE, leaning RIGHT. One large orange
mass near the top (the AI writing the whole letter), then a thin tail of small blue
stubs (the nudges) each answered by medium orange. Reads as: "the AI built this; I
just poked at it."

------------------------------------------------------------------------
User: can you write me a cover letter for a marketing manager role at a startup called Lumen? i'm applying tomorrow

Assistant: Happy to. To make it land, anything specific about your background or why Lumen — or should I write a strong general version you can lightly tweak?

User: uh i've got about 5 years in marketing, mostly social media and some email campaigns. that's really it. just make it sound good

Assistant: Here's a strong draft:

Dear Hiring Team,

I'm excited to apply for the Marketing Manager position at Lumen. With over five years of experience driving marketing initiatives, I've developed a deep expertise in social media strategy and email campaign management that I believe aligns closely with what a fast-moving startup like Lumen needs.

In my previous roles, I led social media efforts that grew engaged audiences and designed email campaigns that nurtured leads through the funnel. I thrive in environments where I can wear multiple hats, move quickly, and see the direct impact of my work on growth.

What draws me to Lumen specifically is the opportunity to help shape a brand at an early, formative stage — to build something rather than maintain it. I'd love to bring my energy and experience to your team.

Thank you for your consideration. I look forward to the possibility of contributing to Lumen's growth.

Sincerely,
[Your Name]

User: oh nice. can you make it a bit longer though? feels a little short for a cover letter

Assistant: Sure — here's an expanded version with an extra paragraph fleshing out your impact and approach:

[...same opening...]

Beyond the day-to-day execution, I take a data-informed approach to marketing — I pay attention to what's actually moving the needle and double down there, rather than spreading effort thin. Whether it's testing subject lines, refining audience targeting, or finding the content formats that resonate, I'm comfortable owning a channel end to end and reporting on what works.

I'm especially comfortable in the ambiguity of an early-stage company, where priorities shift and the playbook is still being written. I see that as an opportunity rather than a challenge...

[...same closing...]

User: perfect. actually can the opening be more catchy? the "I'm excited to apply" thing is so generic

Assistant: Agreed — here's a sharper hook to open with:

"The hardest part of early-stage marketing isn't doing the work — it's knowing which work matters. That's the instinct I've spent five years sharpening, and it's exactly what I'd bring to Lumen as your next Marketing Manager."

Then it flows into the body as before. Want me to drop that in and send you the full thing clean?

User: yes love it, that's so much better. is this good enough to send or should i change anything?

Assistant: It's solid and ready to send. Two optional ways to make it even stronger: (1) swap in one concrete metric — e.g. "grew our Instagram following 40%" or "improved email open rates from X to Y" — because specifics beat adjectives, and (2) name one specific thing about Lumen's product or market to show you researched them. Even one line of each would lift it noticeably.

User: i don't really have exact metrics off the top of my head lol, just leave it as is. can you also write a short email to send it with, attaching the letter?
