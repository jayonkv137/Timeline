# Foundation: Voice and words

## Tone

Calm, plain, exact. COTRACE is a mirror, not a judge: it describes what happened and never grades the person.

## Vocabulary used in the interface

| Say | Not | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Your decision | User requirement | Legend, tooltips |
| AI decision | AI requirement | Legend, tooltips |
| Open question | Slot, open slot | Legend, tooltips |
| Answered | Resolved slot | Legend |
| Share ring | Mass split, influence ratio | Legend |
| Focus | Terminal active node | Legend |
| Pair 4 | Turn 4, P4 (in prose) | Badge, toasts. `P4` only on notches |
| Conversation | Chat log, session | Lists, headers |
| Brain | Knowledge graph, galaxy | Tab, navigation |

Engine words (outcome, requirement, slot, ledger, mass, SHAPER, EXECUTOR) stay in the engine and the specs. They don't appear in the UI unless a tooltip explains them.

## Rules

- Sentence case. Uppercase only as an overline in tooltips.
- Name things by what people recognise, not how the engine computes them.
- Never moralise: no "too much AI", no warnings about reliance, no scores framed as good or bad.
- Empty states say what will appear and how to get it ("Your graph will appear after the first analyzed exchange.").
- Errors say what happened and what to do, without apology filler.
- Numbers: whole masses (no decimals); percentages only in tooltips, rounded.
