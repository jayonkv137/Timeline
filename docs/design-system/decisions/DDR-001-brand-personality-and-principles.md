## 1. Context & Problem Statement
Prior to Phase D1, the repository suffered from fragmented visual worlds (warm paper panel vs obsidian graph prototype vs default shadcn chatbot) and lacked an authoritative strategic anchor. Following Katie Dill's craft manifesto (*"Raise the Ceiling"*), the system required an uncompromising point of view to prevent falling into the "microwave burrito trap" and producing generic "Zombie UI".

## 3. Decisions Codified

### A. Register
Strictly **`product`**. The interface is an operational cockpit and cognitive mirror. Design serves human thought, orientation, and traceback, rather than decorative marketing spectacle.

### B. Brand Personality Sliders (1–10)
- Calm ↔ Energetic: **`4`** (quiet focus, motion reserved for state change)
- Instrument ↔ Companion: **`3`** (precision measuring compass, not an anthropomorphic chatbot)
- Technical ↔ Humane: **`7`** (humane warmth and thoughtful craft over raw debugger telemetry)
- Quiet ↔ Expressive: **`3`** (disciplined restraint; thoughts provide richness)
- Organism ↔ Machine: **`Symbiotic Hybrid`** (living thoughts growing along mathematical precision)
- Observatory ↔ Notebook: **`4`** (astronomical spatial wonder with intimate notebook tactility)

### C. Spatial Mental Model & Zoom Hierarchy
1. **Macro Level 1:** Symbiotic Human × AI 3D Form / Cortex mapping all chats as constellation clusters within an atmospheric containment envelope.
2. **Micro Level 3:** Single-chat graph node structure adjacent to active chat with tabbed workspace continuity.
3. **Traceback Sync:** Bidirectional scroll/scrub synchronization; clicking an atom jumps to the verbatim transcript turn.

### D. Six Binding Strategic Principles
1. *A Mirror, Not a Judge* (Zero moralizing scores or guilt cues).
2. *Every Mark Traces to Its Source* (Verbatim quote provenance for all visual marks).
3. *Geometry Before Typography* (Pre-attentive 250ms comprehension via position/size/hue).
4. *Bidirectional Temporal Synchronization* (Chat scroll scrubs graph; bead click navigates chat).
5. *Stillness First, Motion for Meaning* (Zero ambient looping or idle distractions).
6. *The Engine is Complex; the Picture is Simple* (Mathematical depth masked by spatial clarity).

### E. Persona Review Panel & Ranking Order
1. `graph-glancer` (Primacy 1 — The In-Flow Graph Glancer)
2. `context-recoverer` (Primacy 2 — The Traceback Searcher)
3. `brain-navigator` (Primacy 3 — The Macro Explorer)
