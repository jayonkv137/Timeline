# DDR-000a: Real-Data Design Protocol (Settlement A1)

- **Status:** Ratified
- **Date:** 2026-10-08
- **Decider:** Owner (via D Track Brief §5 Settlement A1)
- **Phase / Step:** D0.S3
- **Affects:** Mockups, Data Access, Privacy

---

## 1. Context
`ui-craft` and general design tooling default to manufactured, synthetic lorem-ipsum fixtures to avoid leaking personal data. However, for the Agency Panel and Co-Creation Brain, synthetic data cannot reveal the cognitive density, varying conversation depths (1 pair to 242 pairs), or verbatim evidence traces of real collaboration.

## 2. Decision
The owner authorizes the **Real-Data Protocol**:
- Design mocks may link and render real processed chats exclusively from `data/chats/` (the 10 Phase E1 corpus chats: `c03`, `c04`, `c05`, `c07`, `c10`, `c16`, `c17`, `c20`, `c27`, `c30` and `golden_01`).
- Mocks strictly read sanitized fixtures from `mockups/captures/*.json`, generated by `scripts/design_system/capture.py`.
- Under no circumstances may mocks or scripts touch private archives (`data/archive/`, `data/exports/`, `data/Takeout*`) or anything flagged `sensitive`.
- Brain-level mocks may use titles, lengths, and task types from `data/corpus/`, with region and link labels explicitly watermarked: _"design fixture, not engine output"_.
