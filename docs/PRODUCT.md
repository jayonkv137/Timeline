## Users

### The Rigorous AI Power User
Builders, software engineers, creative thinkers, researchers, and professionals who use AI continuously and deeply across long, multi-turn conversational projects. 

### Context of Use
Their projects span dozens or hundreds of conversational exchanges with multiple LLMs. Over hours, days, or months, crucial context becomes buried in long transcript scrolls:
- It is difficult to recall what decisions were made, when requirements shifted, or who steered a particular milestone.
- Users often experience either a loss of agency (*"Did the AI steer this off track?"*) or cognitive friction when returning to an older project (*"Where did we leave off and why?"*).

### The Four Canonical Situations & Moments
1. **The Context Recoverer (Traceback):** Returning to a long chat after days or weeks away. The user glances at the adjacent graph to see the entire structural evolution of the chat. Clicking any requirement atom immediately scrolls the chat to that exact verbatim turn.
2. **The Steering & Goal Glancer:** Deep in workflow, the user glances at the adjacent graph node structure to verify cognitive equilibrium through node colors and bead sizes: *"Am I driving the architectural intent, or is the AI unilaterally deciding requirements?"*
3. **The Active Session Tracker:** Working in real time as the chat unfolds. The graph grows dynamically alongside the conversation, with bidirectional scroll synchronization (scrolling up in chat scrubs the graph back in time).
4. **The Cosmic Brain Recaller (Macro-to-Micro Retrieval):** Navigating the vast multi-project 3D Brain territory. The user spots an older project cluster, dives into that chat's atomic graph, recollects the intent, and pinpoints a specific turn to adapt.

## Product Purpose

### What It Does
The Human × AI Co-Creation Brain is an operational thinking environment and cognitive mirror. It translates conversational AI interactions into structured, spatial knowledge:
- At the **Micro Level** (single chat): The adjacent graph node structure renders active goals, requirement satellites, and mutual human vs. AI influence in real time.
- At the **Macro Level** (entire workspace): A Symbiotic 3D Cortex maps all historical chats and emergent topics into an exploratory celestial territory.

### Why It Exists
To elevate human agency in the age of generative AI. By making conversational trajectory, requirements, and decisions visually tangible and spatially navigable, it prevents the cognitive erosion of long text threads and ensures humans remain deliberate co-creators rather than passive consumers.

### What Success Looks Like
A user glances at the interface and within 250 milliseconds understands:
- Where they are in the project trajectory.
- Who steered the recent requirements.
- What decisions were locked.
- Exactly how to jump to any historical claim or requirement with zero hunting.

**Disciplined. Luminous. Truthful.**

- **Calm (1) ↔ Energetic (10): `4`**

- **Instrument (1) ↔ Companion (10): `3`**

  Thoughts and projects grow like living neural constellations, while the measurements, nodes, and tethers behave with mechanical mathematical precision.
- **Observatory (1) ↔ Notebook (10): `4`**  
  Combines the vast, spatial wonder of an astronomical observatory with the intimate, tactile familiarity of a personal notebook.

### Emotional Goals & Boundaries
- **Desired Emotional States:** Calm orientation, empowered agency, spatial clarity, effortless recall, and wonder at the visible architecture of one's own ideas.
- **Strictly Forbidden Feelings:** Guilt, algorithmic judgment, the anxiety of being graded, casino-style score chasing, and cognitive fatigue from blinking lights.

## Anti-References (What We Strictly Reject)

Guided by Katie Dill's craft manifesto (*"Raise the Ceiling"*), we ban the following patterns:
1. **The "Zombie UI" & Microwave Burrito Trap:** Rejecting generic AI templates, default shadcn gray cards, cookie-cutter drop shadows, and hasty "good enough" interfaces that lack distinct intent and soul.
2. **The Sci-Fi Gaming HUD:** Banning fake military telemetry, spinning hexagonal targets, glowing high-tech wireframes, and "Iron Man / Jarvis" tropes. Every mark on our screen represents a real mathematical fact.
3. **The Generic SaaS Analytics Dashboard:** Banning grids of identical cards with big numbers, small labels, and colorful trend arrows.
4. **The Casino Gamification Trap:** Banning streak counters, congratulatory popups, confetti animations, badges, and moralizing labels.
5. **The Sterile Developer Debugger:** Banning monochrome green-on-black terminal outputs or raw JSON dumps that offer no spatial comprehension or human warmth.

## Spatial Architecture & Zoom Ladder Continuity

The interface preserves continuous spatial orientation across 5 zoom levels:
1. **Level 1 — The Macro Brain:** A Symbiotic Human × AI 3D Form / Cortex mapping all historical projects and chats into luminous constellation clusters.
2. **Level 2 — Project Region:** A focused cluster within the cortex, surfacing interconnected chats and shared thematic threads.
3. **Level 3 — Single Chat Graph:** The atomic deliverable sphere with orbiting satellite requirement beads, outcome nodes, and mutual influence tethers.
4. **Level 4 — Turn Pair Drawer:** An inspectable drawer detailing the active outcome, requirements introduced, and deterministic ledger metrics for a single exchange.
5. **Level 5 — Verbatim Transcript:** The raw, authentic human and AI messages anchored to the exact quote.

**Workflow Continuity:** Selecting a project cluster in the 3D Brain seamlessly opens the chat tab alongside its adjacent graph node view.

## Strategic Design Principles (Each With a Test That Can Fail)

### 1. A Mirror, Not a Judge
The interface reflects the dynamic between human and AI with absolute neutrality. It provides objective data, never moral evaluation.
- **Failure Test:** Any UI element displays qualitative judgment (e.g., "Good job", "Low contribution", "You should steer more"), gamified reward badges, or judgmental color valence (red = bad, green = good).

### 2. Every Mark Traces to Its Source
Every visual element on the screen—every bead, tether, node radius, and bar segment—is grounded in a verified engine fact with an exact provenance.
- **Failure Test:** A user clicks a requirement bead or outcome node and the system cannot jump to or highlight the exact verbatim quote in the chat transcript.

### 3. Geometry Before Typography (Treisman Channels)
Core structural truths (who steered, how much was decided, what is active) must be comprehended within 250 milliseconds through spatial position, size, and hue before the user reads a single word.
- **Failure Test:** A user must read a paragraph or inspect a data table just to understand which party influenced a requirement or which outcome is dominant.

### 4. Bidirectional Temporal Synchronization
The chat thread and the spatial graph are two views of the same reality. Time flows identically across both.
- **Failure Test:** Scrolling backwards in the chat transcript fails to scrub the graph state backwards, or clicking a past timeline slot in the graph fails to navigate the transcript.

### 5. Stillness First, Motion for Meaning
Motion is strictly reserved for communicating real state change, temporal transitions, or user-initiated navigation. 
- **Failure Test:** Any ambient looping animation, pulsing glow, or breathing effect continues when the system is idle and the data has not changed.

### 6. The Engine is Complex; the Picture is Simple
The COTRACE pipeline executes complex multi-step Bayesian and algebraic derivations. The user interface must present clean, effortless spatial clarity, never exposing pipeline plumbing.
- **Failure Test:** Raw arithmetic equations, intermediate pipeline logs, or confidence uncertainty formulas clutter the primary visual canvas.

## Accessibility & Sensory Standards

1. **Contrast Floor:** All body typography, labels, and critical indicators must meet or exceed **WCAG 2.2 AA (4.5:1)** against their active background in both Light (Paper) and Dark (Obsidian) modes.
2. **Color Vision Deficiency (CVD) Safety:** Human and AI party indicators must never rely on a red/green axis. Hue separation must remain distinct and unambiguous under Deuteranopia, Protanopia, and Tritanopia simulations.
3. **Hit Target Floors:** All interactive elements maintain a minimum target size of **24 × 24 px** (WCAG 2.2 AA) with invisible hit area padding on sub-10px beads, expanding to **44 × 44 px** on touch surfaces.
4. **Reduced Motion:** If `prefers-reduced-motion` is active, all camera pans, zoom transitions, and layout morphs resolve instantly with zero easing delays.
5. **Performance & Realism Floor:** The 3D WebGL cortex and 2D canvas graphs must sustain **60 FPS** rendering on standard hardware without hogging CPU cycles needed by active chat typing.
